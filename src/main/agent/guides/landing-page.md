# Landing pages

Frame: width 1440, height "fit_content(3200)", layout "vertical", gap 0. Each section is a full-width frame with its own vertical padding (96-128) and a centred inner frame with a max width of 1120-1200.

Story before style: write the headline and section order first, then design. A good arc is promise, how it works, proof, objections, action.

Hero:
- One headline, one supporting line, one primary call to action (plus at most one quiet secondary link).
- Headline 56-80, tight line height (1.05-1.15), max two or three lines. Lead with the outcome for the visitor, not the feature list.
- A strong visual: product shot, scene, or bold typography. Text over images needs an overlay or its own surface for contrast.

Sections:
- Keep one alignment per section; do not mix centred and left-aligned blocks.
- Alternate dense and light sections: a text-led section, then a visual one. Use a dark or tinted band once or twice for emphasis, not every other section.
- Features: three or four, each with a short title and one sentence. Avoid six identical cards; vary with a large feature plus smaller ones, or alternating image and text rows.
- Proof: logos (Simple Icons), a pull quote with name and role, or one big number.
- Final call to action repeats the hero action with a fresh line.
- Footer: quiet, small text, grouped links.

Type: body 16-18 with line height 1.5 and 50-75 characters per line. Centre at most two or three lines of text.

Colour: the call to action is the most prominent element on the page. Do not spend the accent colour on decoration.
